We welcome motivated people to join the lab. Training in mass spectrometry is provided, so prior experience is not required.

## How to apply

Email your curriculum vitae, full publication list and a summary of your previous experience to Dr. Zhao Qian at [q.zhao@polyu.edu.hk](mailto:q.zhao@polyu.edu.hk). Please use the subject line "Postdoctoral application – Your Name" or "Doctoral application – Your Name".

## Postdoctoral Fellow

**Responsibilities**

- Conduct research, analyze results and draft manuscripts.
- Help supervise graduate students and support laboratory management.
- Apply independently for regional, national or international research funding.

**Requirements**

- A PhD, preferably in bioinformatics, or in a related field such as molecular or structural biology, cell biology, physiology, chemical biology, proteomics or pharmacy.
- At least one first-author paper in a Science Citation Index journal, accepted or in press.
- Fluent written and spoken English.
- Initiative and enthusiasm for research.

## PhD Student

**Requirements**

- A bachelor's or master's degree in molecular biology, structural biology, cell biology or bioinformatics; bioinformatics is preferred.
- Enthusiasm for research, self-motivation and a strong sense of responsibility.
- Overseas applicants may need to submit International English Language Testing System or Test of English as a Foreign Language scores.
