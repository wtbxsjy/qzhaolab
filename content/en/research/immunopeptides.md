Cells display short peptides on their surface through human leukocyte antigen (major histocompatibility complex) molecules, and T cells use them to tell healthy cells from infected or cancerous ones. A growing share of these immunopeptides comes from non-canonical sources, including small open reading frames.

## Approaches

- **Immunopeptidomics by mass spectrometry**: isolating peptides bound to human leukocyte antigen molecules and identifying them at scale.
- **Integrated search strategies** that combine proteogenomic databases with mass spectrometry to find non-canonical class I and class II peptides.
- **Translational studies** that evaluate newly found peptides as candidate targets for tumor immunotherapy.
