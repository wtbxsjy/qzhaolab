Thousands of short open reading frames across the genome were long dismissed as non-coding. We now know many of them are translated into peptides and microproteins with real biological functions. Our Peptidein program aims to find these molecules systematically and to understand what they do.

## Approaches

- **Proteogenomics**: building custom sequence databases from genomic and translatomic data so that mass spectrometry can detect peptides absent from reference protein databases.
- **Optimized mass spectrometry workflows**, including data-independent acquisition, for deeper detection of low-abundance alternative proteins.
- **Functional studies** of newly discovered microproteins in immune cells, development and cancer.

## Recent findings

- Small open reading frames and functional microproteins in activated T cells.
- Alternative proteins in mouse cardiac and liver development.
